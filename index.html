Profile

R&D leader integrating quantitative genetics, machine learning, and high-performance statistical workflows to accelerate genetic gain while strengthening portfolio decisions, resource allocation, and risk visibility across complex breeding systems.

Current work spans stability analysis, genomic prediction, multivariate modeling, and software platforms that connect science, operations, and business strategy to improve execution quality and long-term product outcomes.

Current Focus

  • Decision support tools for trial networks and product placement

Strategic Profile

Enterprise-scale R&D leader translating scientific strategy into decision-ready development systems for portfolio advancement across geographies, crops, and distributed teams.

Leads breeding analytics and discovery in Latin America and Africa by integrating science, operations, and business priorities to improve flow, quality, and long-term product success.

Core Leadership Capabilities

  • Strategic and systems leadership: prioritization, roadmaps, decision framing
  • Scientific stewardship: data standards, model governance, evidence packages
  • Field and network design: optimization, environmental classification, modernization
  • People leadership: distributed-team development, coaching, cross-functional influence

Leadership Impact Highlights

  • Led regional transformation of breeding decision systems with KPI-based advancement and placement frameworks.
  • Built process and resource mapping infrastructure to evaluate flow, cost, timeline, and technical risk trade-offs.
  • Improved portfolio quality and commercial focus by modernizing score models across producibility, disease, and market fit.
  • Advanced environmental characterization strategy using phenotype, genotype, and environmental covariates.
  • Scaled technical capabilities through team leadership, analyst development, and breeder training in statistical computing.

Selected Technical and Organizational Contributions

  • Software and systems: Blueprint 2.0, Station app, GenTrends app, Real-Time Analysis app, BT-SAT-R, Crux, eMM3.
  • Scientific output: 1st place G2F Genotype by Environment Prediction Competition (2023); baseline-winning solution in G2F (2025).
  • Recognition: ACE Process Innovation Awards (global and regional), NAPPN Industry Award, Breakthrough Innovation Award.
  • External leadership: ASA symposium chair roles, biometrics community leadership, editor and special issue editor roles.
